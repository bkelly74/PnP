# BRAND STANDARDS MANUAL — MATCHDAY CLUB®

_Working document. Sections 1–4 drafted in Session 001._

## 1. Brand Position
Matchday Club does not sell football accessories. It sells **preparation**.
Every product exists to make footballers better prepared. Premium performance —
built to sit alongside Nike, Castore, Gymshark, On and MAAP. Never a startup look.

## 2. Identity System
- **Corporate Identity** — Shield + MATCHDAY CLUB wordmark.
- **Product Identity** — Shield only (DR-003).
- **Performance Mark** — manufacturing chevron for knit, embroidery, silicone, woven (DR-004).
- The corporate logo is derived **from** the Performance Mark, never the reverse (DR-005).

## 3. The Shield
- Built on a 1000-unit construction grid (DR-010), perfect bilateral symmetry about x=500.
- Flat top, vertical shoulders to y=520, bezier sweep to a single point at (500, 880).
- The chevron reverses out of the shield in Bone.

### Clear space
Minimum clear space = the height of the chevron stroke (130u at the master scale)
on all sides. Nothing intrudes.

### Minimum size
- Digital: shield not below 24px tall.
- Embroidery: not below 25mm tall (re-check stitch density at final size).

## 4. Colour
See `01_Brand/Color/MDC_Color_System.md`. Mono-first; single Pitch accent, sparing.

## 5. Typography  _(pending — DR-008)_
Primary/secondary typeface to be locked, then the wordmark outlined to vector.

## 6. Misuse  _(to draft)_
No stretching, recolouring outside the system, rotation, drop shadows, or outlines.
