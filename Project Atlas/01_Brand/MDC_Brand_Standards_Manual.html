01 · Position

Brand position

The Match Day Club is a monthly subscription box for young footballers (12–17) — premium gear, real nutrition and recovery essentials, curated by players.

"Elevate your game. Every month."

  • Built by players, for players.
  • Transparent value (every item's retail price listed).
  • Parent-safe: no stimulants, no lock-in, age-appropriate.
  • Gamified: streaks, badges, levels.

Identity system

  • Corporate logo — shield + wordmark.
  • Product mark — shield only.
  • One-colour mark — single-ink manufacturing.
Master shield on brand ground #0A0B0D
02 · The mark

The shield

A double-keyline angular shield holding an outlined "MD" monogram, with a green down-chevron in the top notch and an offset two-tone green accent stroke. Not bilaterally symmetric — the right side reads as the "D"; never "correct" it to a symmetric M.

Construction — 1000-unit grid, centre axis

Vector-traced + RDP-regularised from the live artwork. Digital master in 01_Brand/Logo/; straight-edge print master in 08_Exports/Print/.

07 · Packaging

Packaging

Matte black, restrained, tactile. Shield as blind deboss + spot matte-UV; green as a single accent. No gradients — depth comes from finish.

Box lid — matte black, debossed shield, white/foil wordmark

Roll-end tuck mailer + swing tag + seal + tonal tissue. Specs in 03_Packaging/.
