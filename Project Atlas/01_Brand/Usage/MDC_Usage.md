# LOGO USAGE — MATCHDAY CLUB®

Production usage rules for the shield and Performance Mark. Diagrams in this folder
are the authoritative geometry; the Brand Standards Manual references them.

## Clear space — `MDC_Clearspace.svg`
- Minimum clear space on **all four sides** = **X**, where **X = the Performance
  Mark chevron stroke height = 130u** at master scale.
- Nothing — type, rule, image edge, another mark — may enter the keep-out zone.
- Clear space scales with the mark: measure X from the mark in use, never a fixed pixel value.

## Minimum size — `MDC_Minimum_Size.svg`
Applies to the Product Identity (shield only); the Corporate lockup is governed by its
wordmark legibility and sits larger.

| Medium | Floor (shield height) | Note |
|--------|----------------------|------|
| Digital | **24px** | Below this the chevron fills in. |
| Print / embroidery | **25mm** | Re-check stitch density at final size (see Supplier Pack, M2). |

Preferred use is above the floor with breathing room — the floors are limits, not targets.

## Misuse — `MDC_Misuse.svg`
The shield is never:
1. **Stretched** — aspect ratio stays locked.
2. **Rotated** — it always sits upright.
3. **Recoloured** outside the system — Atlas Black / Bone / Pitch only.
4. **Shadowed** — no drop shadows, glows, or bevels.
5. **Outlined** — no added strokes or keylines.
6. **Low-contrast** — keep clear figure/ground separation; never place tone-on-tone.
