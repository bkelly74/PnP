# PRODUCT SYSTEM — THE MATCH DAY CLUB

How the brand and mark apply across the monthly box and its contents. The product *is* the brand
experience, so application is restrained and consistent: **shield only on product** (DR-003), green
as a single accent, matte black everywhere.

## Range architecture
**One subscription, two tiers, monthly themes.**

| Tier | Price (founder) | Contents |
|------|-----------------|----------|
| **Starter** | £15.99/mo | Pro grip socks · sports tape · protein/energy snack · electrolyte hydration · monthly challenge card |
| **Pro** | £24.99/mo | Everything in Starter + recovery item (massage ball/roller) + premium nutrition + bonus accessory (bottle/kit bag) + Pro-only badges |

- **Monthly theme** drives each box (e.g. Hydration, Recovery, Pre-season) — a focus that ties the
  gear, nutrition and the challenge card together.
- **Gamification** is part of the product: streaks, badges and levels (see `Gamification/`).
- Audience: **12–17**. Nutrition is age-appropriate (no stimulants); copy is player-to-player.

## Consumable labels
- **Hydration sachet** (`Labels/MDC_Hydration_Sachet.svg`) — matte black, white shield + `HYDRATION`,
  green flavour flash. Always carry the age/no-stimulant line.
- **Energy bar wrap** (`Labels/MDC_Energy_Bar_Wrap.svg`) — matte black flow-wrap, shield left,
  `ENERGY BAR` + benefit line, green edge bars.
- All consumables: shield only, one green accent, legal/nutrition panel on the reverse (factory template).

## Gamification (`Gamification/`)
- **Badges** (`MDC_Badges.svg`) — circular medallions (shield + glyph + green ring): First Box,
  Hydration, Streak ×3, Captain, Level Up, Recovery. Extend the set per monthly theme; digital +
  printed-sticker versions.
- **Monthly challenge card** (`MDC_Challenge_Card.svg`) — front (shield + "Monthly Challenge"),
  back (this-month checklist + streak tracker). One per box; ties the box to the app/streak.

## Rules
- **Shield only on product**; the wordmark stays for packaging/marketing.
- Green is a single accent (flavour flash, badge ring, streak) — never a fill.
- Match all inks/yarns to the approved Pantone (`04_Supplier/Color`).
- Approve a physical sample per product before bulk.
