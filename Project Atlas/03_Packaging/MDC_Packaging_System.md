# PACKAGING SYSTEM — THE MATCH DAY CLUB

The monthly box is the brand's main physical touchpoint — the unboxing *is* the product. The system
is **matte black, restrained, tactile**: less ink, more confidence. No gradients, no clichés, one
green signal. Every spec is a design intent to confirm with the converter on a physical sample.

## Box
- **Structure:** one-piece **roll-end tuck mailer** (self-locking, no glue), E-flute corrugate for
  protection + a premium snap-shut feel. **Internal ~250 × 190 × 80 mm** (confirm to the packed range).
- **Board / colour:** matte black throughout — black-dyed or full-bleed matte-black print with a
  matte (soft-touch) laminate or varnish. Inside printed black too (no raw brown board on open).
- **Lid mark:** shield centred as a **blind deboss + spot matte-UV** (tone-on-tone, catches light) —
  the premium, ink-light treatment. Wordmark below in white screen, or **matte silver foil** as the
  step-up option. Artwork: `Box/MDC_Box_Lid_Artwork.svg`.
- **Dieline:** `Box/MDC_Mailer_Box_Dieline.svg` is a design dieline (cut = solid, fold = dashed);
  the converter supplies the production dieline matched to their board thickness + 3 mm bleed.

## Swing tag
- 50 × 90 mm, **≥ 350 gsm matte uncoated** black board, white ink, 4 mm eyelet + cord.
- Front: shield only. Back: `MATCH DAY / CLUB` + "Elevate your game. Every month." + QR to site.

## Seal sticker
- Ø 60 mm, matte black, white shield, thin green keyline ring. Seals the tissue — first thing the
  member breaks. Matte stock to match the box.

## Tissue paper
- 1-colour, **tonal** (Ink shield on near-black, or dark-on-bone for a lighter option). Subtle
  repeat, not loud — the reveal is the gear, not the wrapping. `Seals_Tissue/MDC_Tissue_Repeat.svg`.

## Unboxing sequence (designed moment)
1. Matte black mailer, debossed shield catches the light.
2. Open → black interior, tissue sealed with the round sticker.
3. Break the seal → gear laid on tonal tissue; swing tag / challenge card on top.
4. Green appears only as the accent — sticker ring, tag tagline, challenge card.

## Colour & finish
- Ground `#0A0B0D`, Ink/white `#F5F7F8`, Green `#8BC63F` (accent only) — per
  `01_Brand/Color/MDC_Color_System.md`; match spot inks/foils to the approved Pantone
  (`04_Supplier/Color/MDC_Color_Thread_Reference.md`).
- **No gradients on packaging.** Depth comes from **finish** (deboss, matte vs spot-UV, foil), not ink.

## Sustainability
- Recyclable mono-material corrugate; soy/water-based inks; paper tape; no plastic film.
- Right-size the box to the range to cut void fill and shipping footprint.

## Pre-production
Approve a physical **white-sample / plain dieline fit-check** (does the range pack?) and a printed
+ finished sample (deboss depth, matte vs spot-UV contrast, foil) before bulk.
