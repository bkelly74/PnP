# COLOUR & THREAD REFERENCE — THE MATCH DAY CLUB

**Authoritative values are HEX/RGB** (taken from the live brand CSS). CMYK and Pantone below are
**suggested starting points to confirm against a physical Pantone book** under controlled light
(D50). Thread numbers are deliberately left to be matched to the **approved Pantone** with a
physical thread card — do not bulk-order thread off these suggestions alone.

## Master colour table
| Role | HEX | RGB | Suggested CMYK¹ | Pantone candidates (confirm) |
|------|-----|-----|-----------------|------------------------------|
| Ink (near-black) | `#0A0B0D` | 10, 11, 13 | 0/0/0/100 (or rich 60/50/50/100) | Black 6 C, or Neutral Black C |
| Ink reverse / Bone | `#F5F7F8` | 245, 247, 248 | 0/0/0/2 | White / unprinted |
| **Green (signal)** | `#8BC63F` | 139, 198, 63 | ~45/0/90/0 | **368 C / 376 C / 375 C** |
| Green deep (bevel) | `#6AA62E` | 106, 166, 46 | ~58/10/100/8 | 364 C / 369 C |

¹ Naïve RGB→CMYK drifts dull; trust the Pantone match over the CMYK numbers for spot work.

## Thread match (embroidery) — method, not guesswork
1. Get the **approved Pantone** for green signed off first (one of the candidates above).
2. At the factory, lay **polyester** thread cards (e.g. Madeira Polyneon / Isacord — both common on
   sportswear for wash- and chlorine-fastness) next to the approved Pantone under D50 light.
3. Pick the closest thread; record its number here once chosen:

| Brand colour | Approved Pantone | Thread brand | Thread no. | Signed off |
|--------------|------------------|--------------|-----------|------------|
| Green `#8BC63F` | _TBC_ | _TBC_ | _____ | ☐ |
| Ink `#0A0B0D` | Black 6 C (TBC) | _TBC_ | _____ | ☐ |
| Bone `#F5F7F8` | White | _TBC_ | _____ | ☐ |

- Use **40 wt** thread as standard; **60 wt** for small detail (monogram keylines at min size).
- Keep one approved thread number per colour across all factories for consistency.

## Other processes
- **Silicone / pad / screen:** mix to the approved Pantone (spot), not the CMYK build.
- **Knit yarn:** match dyed yarn lot to the approved Pantone; request a lab dip before bulk.
- **Dye lots:** approve a physical lab-dip / strike-off each season; record lot numbers.
