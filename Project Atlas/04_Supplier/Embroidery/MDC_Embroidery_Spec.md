# EMBROIDERY SPEC — THE MATCH DAY CLUB

For the shield mark on caps, beanies, left-chest, sleeves, bags. Artwork:
`01_Brand/Logo/MDC_Performance_Mark.svg` (one-colour) or the two-colour mark with the green accent.
Figures are professional defaults — the digitiser's software gives the true stitch count; confirm
with a sew-out on the **actual garment fabric** before bulk.

## Recommended size
- **Left chest:** 60–75 mm wide.  **Cap front:** 50–55 mm wide.  **Sleeve:** 40–50 mm.
- **Minimum (full detail): 50 mm tall.** Below this, the double keyline and monogram gaps merge —
  either enlarge or switch to the simplified/woven treatment (25 mm floor).

## Digitising
- **Keylines (shield frame + monogram):** **satin columns**. Keep column width **1.2–4.0 mm**.
  Any keyline that maps below ~1.0 mm at final size → bump the size, or render that line as a
  **bean/triple run**. The mark is line-art, so most stitches are satin.
- **Green chevron + accent stroke:** **tatami/fill**, stitch angle ~75° to separate from the keylines.
- **Stitch density:** fill **0.40 mm** (≈4 lines/mm); satin auto by width. Reduce density slightly
  on thick performance knits to avoid bulletproofing.
- **Underlay:** edge-run + centre-run under satin; zig-zag underlay under wider fills. Essential on
  stretch/pile fabrics so the top stitches don't sink.
- **Pull compensation:** +0.2–0.3 mm on satin columns (tall narrow columns pull inward — widen
  slightly; this "push-pull" matters most on the vertical monogram legs).
- **Order:** stitch ink/white keylines, then green accents last to sit cleanly on top.
- **Lock stitches + trims:** lock at every start/stop; minimise jumps across the open shield centre.

## Fabric / hooping (sportswear)
- **Backing:** 1.8–2.5 oz **no-show cut-away mesh** on stretch performance fabric (poly/elastane);
  tear-away only on stable woven.
- **Topping:** water-soluble film on piqué/fleece/knit to stop stitches sinking; remove after.
- Hoop firmly; do not stretch the garment in the hoop (causes registration drift on knit).

## Thread
- **Polyester** (Madeira Polyneon / Isacord) for wash-, UV- and chlorine-fastness on kit.
- 40 wt standard; **60 wt** for the monogram detail at smaller sizes.
- Colours per `04_Supplier/Color/MDC_Color_Thread_Reference.md` (match to approved Pantone).

## Stitch-count estimate (guide only)
Mostly satin keylines + small fills. Rough order of magnitude:
| Size (tall) | Est. stitches |
|-------------|---------------|
| 50 mm | ~7,000–10,000 |
| 65 mm | ~10,000–15,000 |
| 75 mm | ~13,000–19,000 |
Two-colour (with green) adds a colour change + ~1,000–2,000 stitches. Confirm in the digitising software.

## Wordmark
Do **not** embroider the full "THE MATCH DAY CLUB" lockup below a size where `CLUB`'s cap height
≥ 4–5 mm. On product, default to the **shield only** (DR-003).

## Pre-production
Approve a **sew-out on the production garment** (not paper): check registration, the monogram
hollows staying open, hand-feel, and wash-test (40 °C) before bulk.
