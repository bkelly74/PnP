# KNIT SPEC — THE MATCH DAY CLUB

For the mark knitted into beanies, scarves, sock cuffs and knit panels. Knit cannot hold the fine
double-keyline detail, so a **knit-simplified mark** is used at smaller gauges. Confirm with the
mill's gauge and a knit-down sample.

## Gauge & technique
- **Fine gauge (12–14 gg):** the mark reads with care; keep ≥ **40 mm** tall.
- **Standard (7–10 gg):** use the **knit-simplified silhouette** — solid shield + monogram as
  negative space; drop the inner keyline and the green bevel (flat green only).
- **Intarsia** for solid colour blocks (shield body, single green accent) — cleanest, no floats on
  the back, best for larger single placements.
- **Jacquard / Fair Isle** for repeated small motifs; limit to **2 colours per row** and keep
  **floats ≤ 5 stitches** (longer floats snag — tack them).

## Colour
- Ink `#0A0B0D`, Bone `#F5F7F8`, Green `#8BC63F` matched to approved Pantone via lab-dip yarn.
- Keep to **2 colours** in a knit motif where possible; the green is the single accent.

## Minimum motif
- One knit stitch = one "pixel". Below ~40 mm at standard gauge the monogram gaps close — use the
  simplified silhouette or move the mark to a woven/printed label instead.

## Socks (the box's grip socks)
- Knit the shield small at the cuff/ankle in jacquard, **or** apply via the silicone print on the
  sole/cuff (see `Silicone/MDC_Silicone_Spec.md`), **or** a woven heel-tab label.
- Keep motif clear of the high-stretch arch; place on the flatter cuff/ankle zone.

## Pre-production
Approve a **knit-down swatch** at production gauge/yarn: check motif legibility, float length,
hand-feel and stretch recovery before bulk.
