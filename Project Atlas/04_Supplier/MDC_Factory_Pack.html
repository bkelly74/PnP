Yarn / silicone / print

  • Mix silicone & ink to the approved Pantone (spot), not the CMYK build.
  • Knit yarn: match dyed yarn to the approved Pantone; request a lab dip before bulk.
03 · Embroidery

Embroidery

Size & placement

  • Left chest 60–75 mm · Cap 50–55 mm · Sleeve 40–50 mm.
  • Min (full detail) 50 mm tall. Below → simplify or enlarge.

Digitising

  • Keylines (frame + monogram) → satin columns, width 1.2–4.0 mm. Lines under ~1 mm → bean/triple run.
  • Green chevron + stroke → tatami fill, angle ~75°.
  • Density: fill 0.40 mm (≈4 lines/mm); satin auto.
  • Pull comp +0.2–0.3 mm on satin; widen tall monogram legs (push-pull).
  • Underlay: edge + centre run under satin. Lock stitches; minimise jumps.

Fabric / hooping (sportswear)

  • Backing: 1.8–2.5 oz no-show cut-away mesh on stretch poly.
  • Topping: water-soluble film on knit/fleece; remove after.
  • Hoop firm; don't stretch the garment (registration drift).

Thread

  • Polyester (wash/UV/chlorine-fast). 40 wt; 60 wt detail.

04 · Knit & silicone

Knit & silicone

Knit

  • Fine 12–14 gg: mark reads, keep ≥40 mm. Standard 7–10 gg: use knit-simplified silhouette (drop inner keyline + bevel).
  • Intarsia for solid blocks (cleanest). Jacquard ≤2 colours/row, floats ≤5 sts (tack longer).
  • Socks: jacquard at cuff, or silicone on sole, or woven heel-tab. Keep off the high-stretch arch.

Silicone

  • Relief 0.6–0.8 mm. Draft ≥10–15° on all walls.
  • Min raised line ≥0.6 mm at final size → sets 30 mm min.
  • Min web/gap ≥0.6 mm so hollows stay open.
  • Single-colour or 2-colour max; cure + 40 °C wash-test.
Both: approve a physical knit-down / moulded sample before bulk — legibility, open hollows, colour vs Pantone, flex & wash.
