Production specifications for applying the Match Day Club mark across manufacturing methods.
Hand this pack (plus the artwork files) to any factory; every figure here is a **starting spec to
confirm with your chosen supplier's machines and your approved Pantone book** — they are realistic
industry defaults, not a substitute for a factory strike-off / pre-production sample.

## Golden rules (apply to every method)
1. **Product carries the shield only** (DR-003). The full "THE MATCH DAY CLUB" wordmark is for
   corporate/marketing, not stitched/moulded onto small product unless it is large enough to read.
2. **Green is a signal** — one accent. On single-colour processes, render the whole mark in one ink
   (`MDC_Performance_Mark.svg`); add green only where a second colour stop is justified.
3. **Respect the minimum sizes per method** (below). Below them the monogram hollows close up.
4. Always run a **pre-production sample / strike-off** and sign it off before bulk.

## Minimum applied size by method (shield)
| Method | Min height | Notes |
|--------|-----------|-------|
| Embroidery (full detail) | **50 mm** | Thin keylines need room; below this, simplify or enlarge. |
| Embroidery (woven-look / simplified) | 25 mm | Brand floor; drop fine keylines. |
| Woven label | 22 mm | High detail possible; confirm loom resolution. |
| Knit (12–14 gg) | 40 mm | Coarser gauge → use the knit-simplified silhouette. |
| Silicone (heat-applied) | 30 mm | Line width ≥0.6 mm at final size. |
| Screen / pad print | 12 mm | As digital floor. |
