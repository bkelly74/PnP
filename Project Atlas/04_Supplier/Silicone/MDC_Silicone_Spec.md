# SILICONE SPEC — THE MATCH DAY CLUB

For heat-applied / moulded silicone (3D) marks on bottles, bags, caps, woven-label badges and the
grip-sock print. Silicone needs draft and minimum web or fine elements bridge/tear. Confirm against
the moulder's tooling and a physical sample.

## Relief & geometry
- **Relief depth:** 0.4–1.2 mm; brand mark raised **0.6–0.8 mm**.
- **Draft angle:** **≥ 10–15°** on all side walls for clean release from the mould.
- **Minimum raised line width:** **≥ 0.6 mm at final size** — this sets the minimum applied size
  (the monogram keylines must each be ≥ 0.6 mm). At 30 mm tall the keylines are ~0.6–0.8 mm; do not
  go smaller without simplifying.
- **Minimum web / gap between elements: ≥ 0.6 mm** so the monogram hollows and the shield keyline
  channel stay open (don't bridge into a solid blob).
- **Edge border:** ≥ 0.5 mm.

## Colour
- Mix silicone to the **approved Pantone** (spot), per `04_Supplier/Color/MDC_Color_Thread_Reference.md`.
- Prefer **single-colour** (ink-on-light or tonal) or **2-colour max** (add green accent) —
  multi-colour silicone needs tight registration and raises cost/reject rate.

## Application
- **Heat-applied** (HTV-style silicone) for flat zones (bag panels, cap, sock cuff); cure per the
  supplier's time/temp/pressure; wash-test (40 °C) for adhesion.
- **Moulded 3D badge** for bottles/bags where a raised tactile mark is wanted; trim/laser the edge.
- **Grip-sock sole:** silicone dots/pattern already functional; if adding the mark, keep it in the
  flatter cuff zone at ≥ 30 mm and ≥ 0.6 mm lines.

## Pre-production
Approve a moulded/applied sample: check line definition, web gaps staying open, draft release,
colour vs Pantone, and a 40 °C wash + flex test before bulk.
