## Brand colours (authoritative)
- Ink / near-black **`#0A0B0D`** · White **`#F5F7F8`** · Green signal **`#8BC63F`** (≈ Pantone 368 / 376 C — **to confirm**).

## Must-do before bulk
- **Confirm the green against a physical Pantone book**, and match the yarn/thread to it
  (sign-off table is in the Factory Pack PDF).
- Send us a **physical sample** (knit-down + silicone) for approval before production.
- Respect minimum sizes (knit ≥ 40 mm; silicone line ≥ 0.6 mm).
