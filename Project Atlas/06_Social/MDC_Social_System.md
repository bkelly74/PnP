# SOCIAL SYSTEM — THE MATCH DAY CLUB

Bold, dark, player-voiced. Anton headlines, one green signal, lots of black. The feed should look
like the box: matte, confident, ink-light.

## System
- **Ground:** matte black (`#0A0B0D`). Green (`#8BC63F`) as the single accent — one word, one CTA,
  one rule. Never a green background.
- **Headline:** Anton caps, large, one idea per post. Body/label: Archivo, tracked caps for kickers.
- **Mark:** shield top-left (posts) or centred (story/avatar). Clear space respected.
- **Photography:** real players/kit/motion on dark; product on matte black with a single green prop.
  Dark-keyed grade so the UI/type sits on-ground.
- **CTA:** green pill — "Join the Club →", "Join the waitlist", "Swipe up".

## Content pillars
1. **Preparation** — gear/nutrition/recovery tips (player-to-player).
2. **The box** — monthly reveal, what's inside, retail value (transparency).
3. **Progress** — streaks, badges, levels, member milestones.
4. **Founder/story** — built-by-players, academy-trial authenticity.
5. **Parent value** — safety, no lock-in, habits (calmer tone, same look).

## Voice
- Direct, confident, encouraging. Player slang OK; never hype-y or salesy.
- Age-appropriate (12–17) and parent-safe. No stimulant/quick-fix claims.

## Do / Don't
- ✅ One message, big type, one green accent, lots of black.
- ❌ Multiple greens, gradients, busy collages, stock-gym gloss, green backgrounds, tiny logos.

## Accessibility
- Caption every post; keep text contrast AA; don't put small green text on black (use ink).
